Best budget mechanical keyboard?

maria_k: I want a mechanical keyboard under 80 euros for typing at work. Quiet if possible. Ideas?

jonas: The Keychron K2 is great value, and you can get it with brown switches, which are quieter.

lee: Royal Kludge RK61 is cheaper, around 45 euros, but the stabilisers rattle a bit.

sam: If noise matters most, look at silent red switches. The Keychron K2 offers those too.
